Ichiji Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Applies to the Ichiji Android app (package com.ichiji.kana)

The short version: Ichiji does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or sensitive data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. Your learning progress is stored only on your device.

Who we are

Ichiji is an app for learning to read Japanese, one character a day. It is made by an independent developer, referred to here as “we”. Contact details are at the end of this page.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, Ichiji keeps the following on your phone, in storage that only the Ichiji app can read:

WhatWhyWhere
Learning progress: which characters you have learned or marked as already known, your review history and scheduleTo choose each day’s character and schedule reviewsOn your device only
Study activity: the days you studied and your streakTo show your progress and streakOn your device only
Settings: daily pace, reminder time, theme, widget styleTo remember your choicesOn your device only

None of this is personal information such as your name, email, contacts, location or identifiers, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Information we collect

None. Ichiji does not collect any data from you or your device. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, and no third-party code libraries.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer any data to any third party, because we do not collect any. There are no types of parties we share data with.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications (optional): to show one daily study reminder and, if you choose, a quiet lock-screen card showing today’s character. You can decline this permission or turn either feature off in the app’s Settings at any time; the rest of the app still works.
  • Run at startup: so that the daily reminder and the midnight widget refresh are set up again after your phone restarts. It does not read any data.

Ichiji does not request access to the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, accounts or phone calls.

Features that involve your phone’s own services

  • Spoken Japanese. When you tap a character to hear it, Ichiji passes that Japanese text (for example “あめ”) to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone, such as the one from Google or your phone maker. Ichiji sends no information about you. The engine is part of your phone’s system and is covered by its provider’s own privacy policy.
  • Device backup. If you have turned on Android’s backup for your Google account, Android may include Ichiji’s progress and settings in that backup so they can be restored on a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone’s settings.
  • Widgets and lock-screen card. These display today’s character and your streak on your home screen and, if enabled, your lock screen, where anyone holding your phone can see them.
  • Google Play. If you install Ichiji from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installation and crash statistics under the Google Privacy Policy. We only ever see aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google provides to developers.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete all of it at any time by choosing Settings ▸ Start over in the app, by clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings, or by uninstalling Ichiji. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your progress is kept in Ichiji’s private app storage, which Android protects from other apps. Since nothing is transmitted, nothing can be intercepted in transit.

Children

Ichiji is a general-audience educational app. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
